SimpliCoin

A clean, minimal crypto tracker. Live prices, a favorites list, curated news and a simple portfolio, all in one quiet dark interface.

What it did

The market at a glance.

SimpliCoin was built for people who want to check prices quickly without the noise of a full trading app.

01Live market list

Top coins with price and 24h change, sortable by market cap.

02Favorites

Pin the coins you care about to a separate tab for a faster check-in.

03Crypto news

Headlines from sources like CoinDesk and CoinTelegraph, filterable by publisher.

04Portfolio & settings

A simple portfolio view, dark theme and your choice of display currency.
