What it does

Rhythms, not deadlines.

Most reminder apps are built around tasks and overdue dates. Litto treats everything as a life object with its own natural rhythm, so nothing turns red because you missed a day.

01Your whole little world

See what feels fresh, what is around its usual time and what has quietly drifted, with the few things that need you first.

02Every corner of your space

Group things by where they live: bed, desk, floor, wardrobe. Add spaces of your own, like a workspace or kitchen.

03Care for you and your ride

Personal care sits next to your car, scooter or bike, with fuel, oil changes and washes in one place.

04A shelf that remembers your page

Scan an ISBN or search by title, log your page and watch the year's reading add up, without a pages-per-day goal.

No account · No ads

Your little world stays yours.

Everything is stored on your iPhone, with an optional backup through your own iCloud account.
