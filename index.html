03 — How we work

Ship small. Learn fast. Keep going.

A short loop from idea to live product, with real users in the picture as early as possible.

  1. Find a sharp problem

    We start with one clear job to be done and a specific group of people who need it. If we can't explain it in a sentence, it's not ready.

  2. Prototype in days

    A clickable version comes first. It's the quickest way to find out whether the idea holds up before investing weeks into it.

  3. Ship the smallest useful version

    Launch with the core feature polished, not ten half-finished ones. Clean UI, fast load times and privacy by default.

  4. Listen and iterate

    Feedback drives the roadmap. Products that find their audience get more love; the ones that don't get retired honestly.
