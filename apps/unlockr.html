What it does

Put the hard part before the scroll.

Unlockr puts one healthy decision between you and the feed. One minute of guided breathing returns five minutes of access, and harder challenges return more.

01Pick the apps that take your day

Choose individual apps or whole categories through Apple's own Screen Time picker.

02A shield instead of a feed

Opening a locked app shows a quiet screen with one button. No countdown to wait out, no accidental tap-through.

03Four ways to earn

Breathe, squats, push-ups or plank. Reps and form are counted on device by the camera, and nothing is recorded.

04Time that only moves when you do

Earned minutes count down only while the app is on screen, and streaks only count real challenges.
